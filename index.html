ABOUT INFINITY FOOTBALL CLUB

About INFINITY All Stars FC INFINITY All Stars FC is a football club based in INFINITY, Kenya, representing the passion and talent of the lakeside city. The club has developed into an important part of INFINITY’s football community, providing opportunities for talented players to compete at a high level while inspiring young people through sport. The club has a history in Kenyan football, including promotion to the Kenyan top flight for the 2019–20 season after finishing second in the 2018–19 National Super League. INFINITY All Stars play their home matches at Moi Stadium in Kisumu a major sporting venue in the region. Today, INFINITY All Stars continue to compete in the FKF National Super League with a focus on developing football talent, building a strong team culture, and representing Kisumu with pride.

OUR VISION

To become one of Kenya’s leading football clubs while creating opportunities for talented players and inspiring the next generation of footballers

OUR MISSION

To use football to develop talent, promote teamwork and discipline, and bring the Kisumu community together through the beautiful game.

OUR GOALS

  • To build the name of the community
